| 1 | **Fully unsupervised cross-chemistry SoH and RUL prediction via regression-based domain-invariant feature learning with physics-constrained alignment** | Develop a domain-adversarial training framework that learns chemistry-invariant latent representations from raw voltage/current/temperature signals without any target labels, extending the regression-based unsupervised approach of [12] from SoC to degradation prognostics. | 2 | **Real-time-capable PINN surrogate with adaptive multi-fidelity hierarchy for on-board SoC and SoH co-estimation** | Design a lightweight PINN that dynamically selects between a full electrochemical-model branch (high fidelity, high cost) and a reduced-order equivalent-circuit branch (low fidelity, low cost) based on available compute budget, trained via the multi-fidelity hierarchical strategy of [18] but optimized for real-time micro-controller inference rather than offline parameter inference. The novelty is the fidelity-switching mechanism that maintains physical consistency while meeting embedded timing constraints, a direction not explored in [18], [20]. | ★★★ Hard | The draft notes that PINN integration with detailed electrochemical models “is still computationally intensive and has primarily been demonstrated for parameter inference rather than real-time estimation” [18], [20]. No existing PINN work implements an adaptive fidelity hierarchy for on-board deployment; the proposed approach directly fills this gap while leveraging the multi-fidelity training insights from [18]. |
| 3 | **Unified multi-timescale Bayesian framework for joint SoC–SoH–RUL estimation with hierarchical uncertainty decomposition** | Combine a slow-timescale RNN for long-term capacity degradation (SoH, RUL) and a fast-timescale adaptive RNN for instantaneous SoC, both sharing a common encoder, with Bayesian output layers that decompose predictive uncertainty into aleatoric and epistemic components. The novelty is the explicit multi-timescale architecture that simultaneously handles all three quantities with tractable uncertainty across diverse temperature and load profiles, overcoming the separate single-task designs of [4] and [21]. | ★★★ Hard | The draft identifies the lack of “a truly integrated solution that simultaneously handles all three quantities with rigorous uncertainty quantification across diverse operating conditions” as a key gap [4], [21]. The dual-RNN structure of [4] handles two timescales but only for SoC and model parameters; [21] does multi-task SoH/RUL but within a single timescale. Merging both ideas with Bayesian uncertainty layers constitutes a feasible next step. |
| 4 | **Adversarially robust thermal runaway early warning model validated on simulated real-world sensor faults via domain randomization** | Train a ConvTransformer (building on [25]) under a domain-randomization protocol that injects realistic sensor noise, packet loss, and thermal-management perturbations during training, and evaluate on a purposely collected noisy-sensor dataset derived from open multiphysics simulations. The novelty is the systematic robustness assessment of TR prediction under real-world degradation of sensor signals, a validation scenario that existing methods [23], [25] have not addressed. | ★★ Medium | The draft explicitly states that TR prediction methods “have largely been validated on simulated or controlled laboratory data, and their robustness under real-world field conditions … requires further investigation” [23], [25]. Domain randomization is a well-understood technique in computer vision that can be directly ported to battery sensor streams; the main work is in building the noise-injection pipeline and the evaluation protocol, making this achievable with medium effort. |

The novelty is the first demonstration of a single quantized model covering the three diagnostic tasks (SoC, SoH, TR) under the strict memory and compute budgets of a production BMS, extending the lightweight focus of [3], [6], [26] from single-task to multi-task. | ★★ Medium | The draft notes that “achieving a favourable trade-off between model complexity and prediction accuracy across all diagnostic tasks remains an active area of research” [3], [6], [26]. While quantization and knowledge distillation are mature techniques individually, no prior work applies them to a unified multi-task battery diagnostic model spanning SoC, SoH, and TR, nor measures real embedded deployment costs. This gap can be closed with existing toolchains (TensorFlow Lite, CMSIS-NN) and public datasets. |
