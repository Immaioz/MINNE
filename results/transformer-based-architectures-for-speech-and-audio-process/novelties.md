| 1 | **Lightweight self-supervised audio transformer via knowledge distillation** | Distil the ASiT [1] or SSAST-variant [2] teacher into a compact student transformer (e.g., using attention-transfer or contrastive distillation), retaining the local–global representational power while reducing parameters and latency for on-device audio classification. | ★★ Medium | Directly addresses the first open challenge: integrating self-supervised pretraining with efficient architectures. [1] and [2] provide strong unimodal backbones but were not designed for resource-constrained deployment; distilling them yields a practical model for real-time keyword spotting and event detection on edge hardware. |
| 2 | **Cross-modal audio-visual pretraining for sound event localization** | Extend HiCMAE’s [3] hierarchical contrastive-masked autoencoder to incorporate spatial cues (e.g., binaural or microphone-array signals) and evaluate on sound event localization and detection (SELD) benchmarks, beyond the emotion-recognition tasks originally reported. | ★★★ Hard | Fills the gap of cross-modal pretraining being underexplored outside emotion recognition [3]. SELD requires joint audio-spatial understanding and would benefit from the skip-connection and contrastive mechanisms that HiCMAE pioneered, but demands new positional encoding and multi-channel input handling. |
| 3 | **Scaling-law study of latent diffusion transformers for audio generation** | Conduct a controlled scaling analysis (model size, data size, compute) of GenAu-style architectures [6], measuring FAD, IS, and CLAP score across 3–4 model scales (e.g., 100M–1.25B params) to derive empirical scaling coefficients, analogous to those known in NLP and image generation. | ★★ Medium | GenAu [6] demonstrates the benefits of joint data–model scaling but does not characterize the scaling exponents. Such a study would provide reproducible guidelines for practitioners (third open challenge) on how to allocate resources when building large audio generators. |
| 4 | **Unified self-supervised backbone for music and environmental audio** | Pretrain a single transformer on a mixture of music and environmental sound corpora using the ASiT group-masked objective [1][4], then fine-tune on music genre classification, instrument recognition, and environmental sound event detection to test transfer across domains. | ★★ Medium | ASiT [1][4] was evaluated only on speech and environmental sound benchmarks; MelodyTransformer [5] targets symbolic music. A unified backbone would bridge the two surveyed subtopics and reveal whether a common self-supervised strategy suffices for both musical and non-musical audio. |
| 5 | **Real-time ambient sound synthesis via distilled GenAu** | Apply progressive knowledge distillation to shrink the 1.25B-parameter GenAu [6] into a sub-100M student that runs in real time on a single GPU, while preserving perceptual quality (FAD, IS) as measured against the teacher. | ★★★ Hard | Combines the first and third gaps: it makes large-scale audio generation deployable while testing whether scaling-driven improvements survive compression. No work to date has compressed a transformer-based latent diffusion audio generator, making this a high-impact yet challenging direction. |
